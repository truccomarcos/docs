---
title: "Property setup"
description: "Prepare a hotel for its first guest tipping experience."
---

> Use an approved test property for the walkthrough. This sample checklist does not confirm that any property is ready for production.

## Before you begin

Have the property name, branding assets, timezone, and integration details ready. Use an account authorized to edit the relevant settings. Some credentials are restricted by role.

## 1. Set the hotel identity

Add the hotel's name, logo, brand color, and policy links. Confirm that the guest experience clearly identifies the property.

## 2. Connect hotel and payment systems

| Area | Configure | Review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PMS | Opera Cloud or Cloudbeds connection | Reservation lookup for a test stay |
| Card payments | The property's payment gateway | An approved test payment |
| Email | Sender name, sender email, and SMTP settings | A test message and its tip link |

Keep passwords, API keys, and guest details out of documentation and screenshots.

## 3. Choose the guest experience

Configure the default daily tip, branding, and available tipping options. Set the property's IANA timezone and local pre-checkout email hour. For example, America/New_York identifies a timezone, while 10 identifies 10:00 local time.

## 4. Review staff and payouts

Confirm staff access and the intended distribution workflow. Review payout configuration separately: collecting a successful tip does not prove a payout has completed.

## Launch review

- Open the guest flow from a test email link.
- Verify a test reservation through the QR flow.
- Check the enabled payment paths with approved test controls.
- Confirm that the team can see the expected records.

Record actual test results before treating this checklist as complete.

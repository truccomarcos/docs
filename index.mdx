## A small gesture, a meaningful thank-you

P51 Tipping helps hotel guests leave a tip for their housekeeping team. Guests open a secure link from a pre-checkout email or scan a room QR code, confirm their stay, and choose how much to give.

## One platform, three experiences

| Experience | Who uses it | What it enables |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guest tipping | Hotel guests | Review a stay and leave a tip |
| Backoffice | Property teams | Configure the property and review tipping activity |
| Staff dashboard | Housekeeping staff | View personal tips, earnings, and payout settings |

## From guest to team

1. A guest receives an email link or scans the room QR code.
2. The reservation is checked against the property's PMS.
3. The guest selects a tip amount and an available payment method.
4. The platform records the transaction for the property's distribution workflow.

## Connected to hotel operations

PMS integrations support reservation lookup and posting tips to a guest folio. Payment integrations support card payments. Payouts are a separate workflow from collecting the guest's payment.
